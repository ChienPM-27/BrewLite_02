## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, tactile café-tech aesthetic that replaces clinical digital interfaces with the organic warmth, luxury, and comforting ritual of artisanal espresso culture. It harmonizes the depth of dark roasted beans and sweet caramel with velvety steamed milk and modern fintech agility.

### Key Tenets
- **Artisanal Warmth:** Deep espresso tones and buttery cream surfaces cultivate an inviting, sensory atmosphere that removes digital coldness.
- **Organic Modernism:** Rounded pill geometry, soft fluid transitions, and delicate tactile depth blend contemporary Scandinavian café architecture with seamless interaction design.
- **Effortless Clarity:** Clean typographic scales and high-contrast dark roast ink ensure effortless reading during fast morning orders and bright outdoor transit.

## Colors

The color system transitions completely away from cold blues to an organic coffee, caramel, and milk palette.

### Core Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#6F4E37`):** Warm Artisanal Roast. Powers primary interactive controls, dominant CTAs, and active navigation indicators. Hover state transitions to `#4A2E1B`; soft surface tinting leverages `#F5EBE1`.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373`):** Warm Honey Caramel. Accents badges, promo headers, active chips, and highlighted metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#2E8B57`):** Botanical Mint. Grounds the experience with freshness and denotes positive status, eco-packaging indicators, and order completion.
- **Neutral (`#231710`):** Dark Roast Espresso. Replaces harsh synthetic black with rich, high-contrast dark coffee ink for titles, prominent copy, and structural icons.

### Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF7F2` (Creamy White) provides a soft, non-glare foundation.
- **Card Containers:** `#FFFDF9` (Vanilla Milk) delivers soft contrast for interactive elements.
- **Secondary Surfaces & Modals:** `#F3EDE4` (Soft Sand) defines segmented groups and floating sheets.

### Semantic Status Indicators
- **Ready / Sẵn sàng:** `#10D98A` on `#E6FCF3` with text `#065F46`.
- **In Preparation / Đang pha chế:** `#D4A373` on `#FAF2E8` with text `#78350F`.
- **Pending / Chờ thanh toán:** `#D97706` on `#FEF3C7` with text `#92400E`.
- **Failed / Hủy / Lỗi:** `#E05A47` on `#FDF2F0` with text `#991B1B`.

## Typography

The typography features **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing clarity, friendly geometric proportions, and clear rendering of Vietnamese accents.

### Typographic Rules
- **Text Color Hierarchy:** Primary headlines and titles use `#231710` (Dark Roast Espresso); secondary text, ingredients, and modifiers use `#7C6E65`.
- **Prices (VND):** Format with dot thousand separators and the lowercase suffix "đ" (e.g., `45.000đ`, `75.000đ`). Keep price numbers bold (`800` or `700`) for instant visual scanning.
- **Headings Tracking:** Apply negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to `headline-xl` and `headline-lg` to create a dense, editorial header posture.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to an 8px fluid system built for comfortable touch targets and balanced column structures.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 2-column fluid product grid with `0.75rem` gutters and `1rem` outer screen padding. Key CTAs and sticky cart bars remain pinned to the bottom.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 3-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 4-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px`, with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer page margins. Right rail holds live order timelines and checkout drawers.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy relies on warm espresso-tinted ambient shadows and creamy glass panels instead of cold gray drop shadows.

### Shadow Scale
- **Level 1 (Card & Button Rest):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(35, 23, 16, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(111, 78, 55, 0.04)`. Subtly lifts food and beverage tiles above the `#FAF7F2` canvas.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** `0 10px 22px -4px rgba(35, 23, 16, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(111, 78, 55, 0.06)`. Elevates active cards, quantity pickers, and selected filter pills.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Docks):** `0 20px 30px -6px rgba(35, 23, 16, 0.12), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(111, 78, 55, 0.05)`. Suspends checkout bottom sheets and floating ordering drawers.

### Translucency & Glassmorphism
Floating navigation bars and top headers use frosted warm glass: `rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.85)` with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and an ultra-fine border of `#E8DED2`.

## Shapes

The design uses generous pill and high-radius geometries that mirror smooth ceramic coffee cups and organic ingredients.

### Radius Distribution
- **Pill (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used universally for all main action buttons, status pills, category pills, search input containers, and quantity adjustment capsules.
- **Cards & Modals (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`):** Beverage cards, customization sheets, and cart sections use `1.5rem` to `2rem` curvature.
- **Secondary Pickers (`rounded-lg`):** Size selectors and condiment toggles use `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), warm roasted brown background (`#6F4E37`), white label (`#FFFDF9`), Level 1 shadow. On hover, background shifts to `#4A2E1B` with Level 2 shadow. Active touch downscales slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Warm creamy caramel surface (`#F5EBE1`), roasted espresso text (`#231710`), zero border. Shifts to `#EDE0D2` on hover.
- **Outlined / Tertiary:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E8DED2`, dark roast text (`#231710`). Turns to `#F5EBE1` on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #F0E7DC`, corner radius `1.5rem`, Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Media:** Product imagery features a soft top-rounded crop with gentle hover zoom.
- **Body:** Beverage title in `#231710`, volume/notes in `#7C6E65`, price rendered in bold `#6F4E37` with `price-md`. A floating `+` button in `#6F4E37` sits at the bottom-right corner for 1-tap addition.

### Segmented Size & Sugar Selectors
- Outer pill container set to `#F3EDE4` with `0.25rem` padding.
- Internal option buttons: Inactive options use `#7C6E65` text; selected choice shifts to pure `#FFFDF9` with Level 1 warm shadow, `#6F4E37` text weight, and complete pill roundness.

### Category Filters (Pill Carousel)
- Inactive pills: Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1px solid #E8DED2`, label `#3B271D`.
- Active pill: Background `#6F4E37`, border `1px solid #6F4E37`, label `#FFFDF9`, with soft caramel drop shadow.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Text Inputs:** Pill container (`rounded-full`), background `#FFFDF9`, border `1.5px solid #E8DED2`, placeholder text `#A89C94`. Active focus shifts border to `#6F4E37` with a subtle `#F5EBE1` outer halo ring.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Encapsulated pill in `#F3EDE4`. Left and right round action icons with center count in `#231710` (`label-lg`). Dim decrement button when quantity equals minimum.
